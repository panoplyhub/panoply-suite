# PanoplySuite_RouteData - NOTICE

PanoplySuite_RouteData is travel data for Panoply Suite's route planner (0.6.0). It is generated by
Panoply Suite's own `tools/build_routedata.py`. Beyond the sources listed below, it contains no code, art,
guide text or data from any other add-on or website.

## Datasets and sources

| Dataset | File(s) | Source | License / terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight nodes (name, position, faction by flight mount) | Era.lua / Forever.lua `RD.nodes` | Client DB2 table TaxiNodes, exported as CSV by wago.tools for build 1.15.9.70576 (Era) and 1.60.1.70707 (WoW Forever) | Game client data tables (facts: ids, names, coordinates) |
| Flight legs (from, to, length, simplified trail) | `RD.legs` | Client DB2 tables TaxiPath and TaxiPathNode (same builds); trail simplified by our own code | Game client data tables |
| Flight master per node | `RD.nodes` (npc, name) | VMaNGOS world database: creature spawns with the flight-master flag, nearest to the node | VMaNGOS (GPL-2.0-or-later) |
| Ships and zeppelins (stops, departure interval, ride times) | `RD.transports` | VMaNGOS `transports` (period) and `gameobject_template` (path id); stops and stop delays from TaxiPathNode; ride times computed by our own code | VMaNGOS (GPL-2.0-or-later) / client data |
| Deeprun Tram | `RD.transports` (kind "tram") | Portal positions from VMaNGOS `areatrigger_template` (2173, 2175); car cycle and ride time from the client table TransportAnimation (objects 176080-176085). Loading-screen time (8 s each), platform walk (15 s) and 3 cars per track are our own hand-authored estimates, to be calibrated in game | VMaNGOS (GPL-2.0-or-later) / client data / own |
| Darnassus / Rut'theran portal | `RD.transports` (kind "portal") | Trigger and arrival points from VMaNGOS `areatrigger_template` and `areatrigger_teleport` (527, 542); the few seconds of stepping through are our own estimate | VMaNGOS (GPL-2.0-or-later) / own |
| Innkeepers and hearthstone bind names | `RD.inns`, `RD.bind` | VMaNGOS creature spawns with the innkeeper flag; inn names from VMaNGOS `areatrigger_tavern`; zone names from the client UiMap table | VMaNGOS (GPL-2.0-or-later) / client data |
| Road graph (named road points, road links, no-go areas for lakes, cliffs and mountain walls) | `RD.roads` | Our own hand-authored zone files in `tools/roadgraph/era/` with WoW Forever overrides in `tools/roadgraph/forever/`, checked by our own `tools/roadgraph/roadgraph.py`. Points were placed by hand in zone map percent; town and landmark anchors from the client tables AreaPOI and WorldMapOverlay (same builds) and from VMaNGOS NPC spawn positions. VMaNGOS patrol paths were shown only as an authoring hint and are not copied | Own data / client data / VMaNGOS (GPL-2.0-or-later) |
| Zone exits (where a zone line of the road graph leaves its zone, and the zone it leads to) | `RD.exits` | Our own hand-authored zone lines in `tools/roadgraph/` (`x <node> <uiMap>:<node> zone`), read at the node's position; the next zone is the other end of the line. In WoW Forever a zone whose road graph is dropped (Mulgore, Redridge Mountains, Stormwind City, Eastern Plaguelands) gets its way back derived from its neighbour's exit: the same world spot on its own map, held to the map's border | Own data |
| Zone rectangles | `RD.rects` | Client DB2 tables UiMap and UiMapAssignment (same builds). WoW Forever also lists the Darkspear Islands battleground map (UiMap type 6), so a place on it has a position | Game client data tables |
| WoW Forever's new boats and skycutters (Zephras Isle and Dalaran, Zephras Isle and Mulgore, Steamwheedle Port and Powderfuse Port, Auberdine and Stormwind Harbor) | Forever.lua `RD.transports` (ids 800000 + the TaxiPath id) | Stops and stop delays from the client table TaxiPathNode (paths 11398, 11457, 11391, 11616). Names, the side that uses each and the word "skycutter" are our own, from the notes in `tools/build_routedata.py`. The tables carry no speed and no timetable: the speed is the median speed of the classic boats and zeppelins (from the VMaNGOS timetables), the departure interval is the round trip at that speed plus the stops. Both are our own estimates, to be timed in game | Client data / own estimates |
| Named places for the Go to search (new zones, the battleground, named areas inside the new zones, new dungeons) | Forever.lua `RD.places` | Client DB2 tables UiMap, UiMapAssignment, Map, AreaTable, UiMapXMapArt and WorldMapOverlay (build 1.60.1.70707). An area's spot is the middle of its box on the zone's map art. No door is known for a new dungeon: it points at the outdoor area that carries its name (City of Dalaran: the Dalaran area, our own link) or at the middle of that area's zone | Game client data tables / own link |
